AMD Display Library (ADL) SDK
https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/display-library

Nexum does not ship any ADL file. Its hardware agent declares a small, read-only subset
of the ADL functions by hand (nexum-agent/Tuning/AdlNative.cs, written from the ADL SDK
headers and documentation) and calls the atiadlxx.dll that the installed AMD graphics
driver provides in System32. The notice below is the licence text from the header
include/adl_sdk.h of the ADL SDK (upstream commit 10d614c, 2025-04-10), reproduced as
that licence requires.
